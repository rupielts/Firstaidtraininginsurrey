What this website collects

This website does not ask for your name or contact details. When you click a course button, a service called Web3Forms sends us an alert with the course name, the page you were on and the time. The button then opens our Google Form.

Registration

The Google Form asks for the details we need to register you. Google processes what you submit. We use it to contact you about your course and to keep class records.

Fonts

Fonts load from Google Fonts, which may receive your IP address.

Your information

You can ask to see, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you. Email info@rupielts.com, call 604-825-8882 or visit 112-8232 120 St, Surrey, BC. We handle personal information under BC Personal Information Protection Act.

Terms

Course details, prices and WorkSafeBC rules can change. Confirm your workplace requirement with your employer or WorkSafeBC before you register. This website is not operated by WorkSafeBC. We teach Red Cross courses as a Canadian Red Cross Training Partner.
