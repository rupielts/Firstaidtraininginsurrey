First aid course questions, answered

Quick answers about OFA 1, OFA 2, Red Cross courses and WorkSafeBC.

Canadian Red Cross Training Partner. Our courses are accepted by WorkSafeBC as Basic (OFA 1) and Intermediate (OFA 2) First Aid.

What is the difference between OFA Level 1 and OFA Level 2?

OFA Level 1, now Basic First Aid, is a one-day course. OFA Level 2, now Intermediate First Aid, is a two-day course with deeper training.

How long are the courses?

Emergency First Aid (OFA 1 / Basic) takes one day. Standard First Aid (OFA 2 / Intermediate) takes two days.

How much do the courses cost?

OFA 1 (Basic) costs $75 and OFA 2 (Intermediate) costs $130.

How do I register?

Click the OFA 1 or OFA 2 button on any page to open our registration form, or call 604-825-8882.

Is a Red Cross certificate accepted by WorkSafeBC?

WorkSafeBC accepts Red Cross Emergency First Aid with CPR/AED as Basic First Aid and Red Cross Standard First Aid with CPR/AED as Intermediate First Aid. Confirm your workplace requirement with your employer.

What are OFA Level 1, 2 and 3 called now?

Basic, Intermediate and Advanced First Aid, since November 2024.

How long is a certificate valid?

Three years. To renew, take the course again.

Do you offer group training?

Call 604-825-8882 to ask about group bookings for your team.

Where are you located and when are you open?

We are at 112-8232 120 St, Surrey, BC, open Monday to Friday, 9 am to 8 pm.
