OFA Level 1 and 2 are now Basic and Intermediate

In November 2024 WorkSafeBC replaced the OFA Level 1, 2 and 3 names with Basic, Intermediate and Advanced First Aid. Valid OFA certificates are still accepted as equivalents, and many people still search the old names, so you will see both here.

Common questions

What is OFA Level 1 called now?

WorkSafeBC renamed OFA Level 1 to Basic First Aid and OFA Level 2 to Intermediate First Aid in November 2024. Many people still use the old names, so we list both.

Is Red Cross Emergency First Aid the same as OFA 1?

WorkSafeBC accepts Red Cross Emergency First Aid with CPR/AED as equivalent to Basic First Aid, formerly OFA Level 1.

Is Red Cross Standard First Aid accepted as OFA 2?

WorkSafeBC accepts Red Cross Standard First Aid with CPR/AED as equivalent to Intermediate First Aid, formerly OFA Level 2. Check what your employer requires.

How long is a first aid certificate valid?

Three years. To renew, take the course again.
