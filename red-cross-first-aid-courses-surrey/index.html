Red Cross or WorkSafeBC: what is the difference?

The Canadian Red Cross develops and certifies these courses. WorkSafeBC is the BC regulator that decides which certificates are accepted at work. Because WorkSafeBC accepts Red Cross Emergency First Aid as Basic and Standard First Aid as Intermediate, one Red Cross course can meet your workplace requirement. Confirm the level with your employer, or see OFA 1 vs OFA 2. Red Cross certificates are valid for 3 years.
