About RUPIELTS First Aid Training

A Canadian Red Cross Training Partner in Surrey, BC.

Canadian Red Cross Training Partner. Our courses are accepted by WorkSafeBC as Basic (OFA 1) and Intermediate (OFA 2) First Aid.

We teach Red Cross Emergency First Aid and Standard First Aid with CPR/AED, the courses WorkSafeBC accepts as Basic (OFA 1) and Intermediate (OFA 2) First Aid.

Our classroom is at 112-8232 120 St, Surrey, BC. We are open Monday to Friday, 9 am to 8 pm. Courses are $75 for OFA 1 and $130 for OFA 2.

Questions? Call 604-825-8882 or visit our contact page.
