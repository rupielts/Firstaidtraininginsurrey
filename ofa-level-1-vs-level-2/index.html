OFA Level 1 vs OFA Level 2: which first aid course do you need?

OFA Level 1 is now called Basic First Aid and OFA Level 2 is now Intermediate First Aid. Both are one-day courses. Basic is $75 and Intermediate is $130. The WorkSafeBC requirement at your workplace decides which one you need.

How to choose

Ask your employer which certificate your workplace needs. WorkSafeBC sets the level using the number of workers on a shift, the hazard rating of the work and the distance to medical care. In general a higher-level certificate also meets a lower-level requirement, but confirm this with your employer first. If you want first aid skills for personal reasons, either course works. Choose Intermediate for deeper training.

What about OFA Level 3?

OFA Level 3 is now called Advanced First Aid, a 70-hour course. We teach the Basic and Intermediate levels.
