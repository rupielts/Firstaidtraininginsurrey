Terms and conditions: blended Red Cross first aid training

Our Red Cross first aid courses are blended: you complete the theory online, then the practical portion in class. Please read these terms before you register.

How the blended course works

  1. A few days after you register, you will get an email from the Red Cross to create your online account.
  2. After creating the account, complete the theory portion online on the Red Cross website.
  3. When the theory portion is finished, you will be issued a completion certificate.
  4. Bring that certificate to class on your course day to show you completed the theory. You then complete the practical portion in class.
  5. If you do not bring the theory certificate to class, you will not be able to complete the practical portion and your first aid certificate will not be issued.

Class timing and location

Class time is 9:30 AM to 6 PM at 112-8232 120 St, Surrey, BC. Please do not book if you cannot come on time. If a student does not come or comes late, the fee cannot be refunded.

Payment

Your seat is confirmed only when the fee is paid. Pay by e-transfer to rupielts@outlook.com within 2 hours of filling in the registration form, or your seat will be released to the next candidate. Course fees are $75 for OFA 1 (Basic) and $130 for OFA 2 (Intermediate).

Refunds and changes

The fee is non-refundable, and seats cannot be postponed to a later date.

CPR mask

Bring your own CPR mask, or buy one at the course location for $10. The mask fee is not included in the course fee.
