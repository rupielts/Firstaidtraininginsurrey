WorkSafeBC first aid requirements for BC workplaces

BC employers must provide first aid supplies, trained attendants and transportation for injured workers. The certificate level an attendant needs depends on the workplace, and WorkSafeBC now uses the names Basic, Intermediate and Advanced First Aid.

How the required level is decided

WorkSafeBC looks at the number of workers on a shift, the hazard rating of the work and how far the workplace is from medical treatment. Check the current requirements on the WorkSafeBC website or ask your WorkSafeBC contact before you book training.

Red Cross courses WorkSafeBC accepts

WorkSafeBC accepts Red Cross Emergency First Aid with CPR/AED as Basic First Aid and Red Cross Standard First Aid with CPR/AED as Intermediate First Aid, with issue-date conditions. See the official lists of accepted Basic and Intermediate certificates. Last checked November 9, 2026.
