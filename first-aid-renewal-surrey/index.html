First aid renewal in Surrey, BC

BC first aid certificates are valid for three years. To renew a Basic (OFA 1) or Intermediate (OFA 2) certificate, you take the course again.

How renewal works

WorkSafeBC says all first aid certificates are valid for three years. Renewing a Basic or Intermediate certificate (formerly OFA 1 or OFA 2) means retaking the training course and meeting the exam requirements. A valid OFA Level 1 or 2 certificate issued before November 1, 2024 stays valid until its expiry date and counts as Basic or Intermediate.

Which course to book

Renewing OFA 1 / Basic: book OFA 1, $75. Renewing OFA 2 / Intermediate: book OFA 2, $130. If your certificate has already expired, the steps are the same: take the course again.

Renew before it expires

Book a few weeks before your expiry date so you stay covered at work. Not sure which level you hold? See OFA 1 vs OFA 2 or what the new names mean. Last checked against WorkSafeBC on November 12, 2026.
